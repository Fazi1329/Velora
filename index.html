COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Clear facts regarding our disclosed purchasing-agent role, pricing rules, shipping logistics, and WhatsApp order flow.

1. How do I place an order with VELORA? expand_more
Browse our catalogue and find the product you wish to purchase. Click “ORDER ON WHATSAPP” on any product card or detail drawer. This generates a prefilled message to our official WhatsApp (+966 57 316 4981) containing the exact product name, SKU, and spreadsheet price. We then personally confirm availability, delivery address, shipping rates, and payment instructions.
2. Why does VELORA order through WhatsApp? expand_more
Because personal agency services require absolute clarity. WhatsApp allows us to answer questions about sizing, share live high-resolution photographs from suppliers, verify exact destination shipping costs, and ensure you are 100% satisfied before any payment is transferred.
3. Is VELORA the manufacturer or retailer? expand_more
No. VELORA operates as an independent disclosed purchasing agent and product-sourcing intermediary. We coordinate between you and our vetted supplier network. Depending on the agreed transaction, the supplier may ship the product directly to your delivery address.
4. Are the displayed prices genuine spreadsheet prices? expand_more
Yes. All 55 prices in our catalogue are preserved exactly as supplied in our supplier’s price list (e.g., $150 USD, $200 USD, $100 USD). We do not add hidden markups or silently replace prices, and there are NO agent fees (0% commission). You only pay the listed catalogue price plus actual carrier shipping.
5. When is an order considered confirmed? expand_more
An order is never confirmed simply by clicking a WhatsApp button. An order is only confirmed once product availability is verified with the supplier, total charges (item catalogue price and direct shipping, with zero agent fees) are confirmed in writing, and payment has been arranged directly with your agent.
6. How do I receive payment details and are there any agent fees? expand_more

Payment details are provided exclusively by contacting your VELORA agent directly on WhatsApp (+966 57 316 4981). We support verified PayPal commercial invoices (with buyer protection) and institutional bank wire transfers. We never publish static checkout links publicly in order to protect client transactions from fraud.

Furthermore, there are absolutely NO agent fees (0% commission). You only pay for your items at the genuine catalogue price plus actual carrier shipping to your address.

7. How is shipping handled and how long does it take? expand_more
Shipping depends on destination country and courier availability (DHL Express, FedEx Priority, Aramex, or local postal carriers). Typical international delivery ranges between 4 to 9 business days following supplier dispatch. Direct tracking codes are provided once shipped.
8. What are the return and cancellation terms? expand_more
Cancellations may be requested at any point prior to supplier dispatch. For returns, items must be in original unworn/unused condition with security seals intact. Because VELORA acts as your purchasing agent, we assist in facilitating return claims directly with the respective supplier according to their return policy.
